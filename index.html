About me

Hello, I'm Liangbin.

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Computer Science and Technology at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, South China University of Technology (SCUT).

My academic interests lie at the intersection of computer vision and multimodal machine learning. I am particularly interested in video understanding, temporal grounding, and methods that enable vision-language models to process long or streaming videos efficiently.
